# Phase 13 Sol-Astra Blue Comparator

Date: 2026-09-25

## Result

GPT-6 Astra corrected the exact frozen blue M2 semantic failure from GPT-6 Sol.
The comparator reused the same request body content, two images, packet ID,
structured-output schema, provider route, Flex service tier, and medium reasoning
effort. No prompt, evidence, or action option changed.

| Model | Decision | Correct | Latency | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| GPT-6 Sol | hold | no | 4.327 s | $0.00401925 |
| GPT-6 Astra | lift | yes | 5.301 s | $0.01749625 |

Astra was about 4.35 times more expensive on this packet and 0.97 seconds slower.
This single comparison supports Sol as the routine default and Astra as a targeted
candidate for visually ambiguous attribute or identity boundaries. It does not
establish a general Astra advantage or a validated escalation router.

## Physical Follow-Up

After observing Astra's decision, one physical follow-up was declared. It is not a
blind preregistered semantic comparison and does not revise the failed Sol cohort.
The immutable Astra packet was bound to a fresh seed-23 blue-can fixture execution.
It completed 30/30 actions and independent legal RGB-D/proprio verification passed:

- grasp-center vertical motion: 50.014 mm;
- paired depth pixels: 230,400;
- median absolute depth change: 0.0136 mm.

Thus the model decision difference translated into verified physical progress with
the same motor backend. External clearance remained unknown and
`motion_qualified=false`.
