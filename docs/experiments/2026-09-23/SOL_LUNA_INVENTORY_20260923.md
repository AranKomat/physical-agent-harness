## Qualitative Check And Decision

Reviewed three-way box overlays for the initial view and late sequence 768.
On the late view all three localized the red object on the table, but GLM called
it `radio`, Sol called it `camera`, and Luna used uncertain `electronic device`.
Luna called the initial glazed door a window and the low cabinet a bench, with
the latter description acknowledging cabinet ambiguity. Sol's initial cabinet
box covers only a small fragment despite receiving category credit. These examples
show why category totals do not establish localization or actionable identity.

Keep GLM as the current occasional inventory candidate. Luna is cheaper but does
not justify replacement on this six-view sample: slower replies and 2/6 invalid
geometry packets. Sol remains the executive/verifier default; this inventory test
does not measure executive reasoning, motor control, recovery or Astra superiority.
Use occasional matched Astra diagnostics separately; Codex transport is a separate
experimental condition, not interchangeable with API inference.
